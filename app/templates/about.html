A platform built by seafarers, for seafarers.

MARADTEST.COM is a specialised platform for seafarers preparing to defend their certificate of competency before the Bulgarian Maritime Administration of the Republic of Bulgaria — marad.bg.

The project was developed by maritime professionals with many years of sea service and in-depth knowledge of the examination material, the requirements of the Bulgarian Maritime Administration — marad.bg and the realities of the maritime profession.

What we offer

The platform covers the full range of modules and questions included in the official test-exam plan of the Bulgarian Maritime Administration — marad.bg. The questions are organised by topic and level, so candidates can work methodically through all of the material — from the basics to the specialised modules.

Besides the classic study mode, we also offer a realistic exam simulator — the same conditions, the same time limit, the same structure. No surprises in the exam room.

Official content

All questions and test modules on the platform are based on the officially approved materials of the Bulgarian Maritime Administration — marad.bg. The content is updated whenever the exam programme changes.
