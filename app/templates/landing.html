Frequently Asked Questions
Answers to the most common questions
How do I get access to the platform?+
Sign up with a promo code or choose a plan. After payment you get instant access to all tests and simulators.
What tests does the platform offer?+
Tests for the deck and engine departments at all levels — Operational and Management Level, following the official MA program.
What is the simulator?+
60 questions with a 60-minute timer — just like the real exam. Passed if you score under 10% wrong answers.
Can I try it before I pay?+
Yes! Click "Demo" and you'll get 1 free test from the deck department and 1 from the engine department.
How does the "Mistakes" mode work?+
It tracks questions you got wrong in the last 2 tests. Questions are removed only once you answer them correctly in consecutive tests.
