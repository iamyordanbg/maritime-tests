Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. WHAT ARE COOKIES

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the platform to remember certain settings and ensure that its features work correctly.

2. WHAT COOKIES WE USE

MARADTEST.COM uses only functional cookies that are necessary for the platform to work. No advertising, tracking or third-party analytics cookies are used.

Cookie Purpose Duration Type
session Keeps the user's session active after logging in to the platform Until the browser is closed / logout Functional
sidebarCollapsed Remembers whether the side menu is collapsed or expanded 1 year Functional
theme Remembers the selected colour theme (light / dark) 1 year Functional
remember_token Supports the “Remember me” option at login 30 days Functional

3. WHY YOUR CONSENT IS NOT REQUIRED

Under Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy) and the provisions of the GDPR, functional cookies without which the platform cannot work properly are exempt from the requirement for explicit consent. They are not used to track or profile users.

4. THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

If you use the “Log in with Facebook” option, Facebook may place its own cookies on your device in accordance with its privacy policy. MARADTEST.COM has no control over these cookies. More information: facebook.com/policy/cookies

When payments are processed through Stripe, technical cookies required for the security of the transaction may be set by Stripe.

5. HOW TO MANAGE COOKIES

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Please note that blocking functional cookies may disrupt the platform — for example, automatic login and saved settings will not work.
