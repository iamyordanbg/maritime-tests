Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. DATA CONTROLLER

MARADTEST.COM is the controller of personal data within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

Contact: info@maradtest.com

2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

2.1 Registration data: email address, password (encrypted), name (optional).

2.2 Usage data: history of completed tests, results, date and time of access.

2.3 Technical data: IP address (hashed), browser type, device.

3. PURPOSE OF PROCESSING

3.1 Providing the subscription service.

3.2 Processing payments.

3.3 Improving the platform.

3.4 Sending system notifications (and, with explicit consent, marketing messages).

4. LEGAL BASIS

4.1 Performance of a contract (subscription) — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4.2 Legal obligation — Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

4.3 Legitimate interest — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4.4 Consent — for marketing messages.

5. DATA RETENTION

Personal data is stored for the duration of the subscription and for up to 2 years after it ends, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

6. SHARING WITH THIRD PARTIES

6.1 Stripe Inc. — payment processing.

6.2 Brevo (Sendinblue) — sending emails.

6.3 Railway — hosting of the platform.

6.4 Meta Platforms Inc. (Facebook): If you choose to use the “Log in with Facebook” option, we receive access to your name, email address and profile picture provided by Facebook. This data is used solely to create and manage your profile on our platform. We do not share your data with Facebook, other than the technical information necessary to verify the authenticity of the login. You can remove our access to your Facebook profile through the security settings of your Facebook account.

Data is not sold to third parties.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erasure (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent

To exercise your rights: info@maradtest.com

8. COOKIES

The platform uses only functional cookies required for the session to work. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

9. SECURITY

Data is stored encrypted. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. The connection is protected with SSL/TLS.

10. COMPLAINTS

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP) of Bulgaria: www.cpdp.bg

11. CHANGES

If this policy changes, you will be notified by email.
